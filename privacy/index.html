1. Information we collect

Account and profile

If you create an account, we store your email address, display name, Firebase user ID, subscription and purchase status, and the profile you fill in: diet type, allergies, intolerances, ingredients to avoid, goals, height, weight, age, gender, activity level and nutrition targets.

Health information you enter

If you add them, we store health conditions, medications (with dose and frequency), supplements, blood-work results, menstrual cycle information, meal and scan history, workouts and body measurements. This information is stored on our servers (Firebase) with your account.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, the app reads these Health data types: nutrition totals (energy, protein, carbohydrates and fat), steps, active energy, exercise and stand time, workouts, height, weight, date of birth and biological sex. It writes the nutrition entries (energy, protein, carbohydrates, fat, fiber, sugar and sodium) and workouts you log. Health data is read on your device when a feature needs it and is not saved to your Yammoing account or our servers. Apple Health values are included in AI requests only if you turn on the Apple Health category in AI data-sharing settings (see section 3). We do not use HealthKit data for advertising, sell it, or use it for research.

Photos and other content

We process photos you take or choose for barcode and label scanning, meal recognition, product contributions and profile images, along with shopping lists, favorites, fridge items, notes, searches and AI chat messages.

Location

Only while the app is open and only if you allow it, the app uses your location to find nearby stores and restaurants, look up air quality, and, if the "Attach Location to Scans" setting is on, save a location with scans and meals. iOS may give the app precise location. Unless you turn on "Store Exact Location", saved coordinates are rounded to about 11 metres; a place name, city and region may be saved with them. You control location access in iOS Settings and in the app's Privacy & Data settings.

Device and technical data

Firebase services that run the app (Authentication, App Check, Firestore, Cloud Functions and Storage) process technical data needed to authenticate you and protect the service, such as your user ID, an App Check attestation, IP address and request metadata. Our Cloud Functions write operational logs that contain your user ID, subscription tier, request sizes and, for purchases, product and transaction IDs. They do not log your prompts, photos or coordinates.

What we do not collect

Yammoing does not include Firebase Analytics or any other analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDK, and does not collect usage analytics or crash reports.

2. How we use information

  • Operate, secure and troubleshoot Yammoing.
  • Personalize nutrition scores, recommendations, meal planning and fitness features.
  • Process the barcode, label, meal-photo, place, air-quality, food-database and AI requests you make.
  • Synchronize your account data and selected photos across your devices.
  • Manage subscriptions, enforce usage allowances, and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Research, only if you opt in (see section 6).
  • Meet legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3. AI features

Yammoing's AI features use a third-party AI provider, called only from our servers; the app never contacts the provider directly. The provider in use is named on the AI data-sharing screen in the app, which asks you again if it changes. Nothing is sent to the provider unless you use one of these features:

  • Meal photo recognition sends the meal photo. Recognition is not done on your device.
  • Label analysis sends the photo of the label.
  • Nutrition questions send your question and the names, calories and macros of the foods it is about.
  • AI nutritionist, trainer and recipe features send your message, your last few messages in the conversation, and context about you so the answer fits your goals.

Minimal by default

Your name and email are never included in AI requests. By default, the AI nutritionist, trainer and recipe features include the context they need to give safe, relevant answers:

  • your profile: diet type and preferences, health goal, nutrition and macro targets, activity level and exercise habits, height, weight and target weight, age and sex;
  • safety information: allergies, intolerances, ingredients to avoid, custom restrictions and listed health conditions (these are always included so the answer does not suggest something unsafe for you);
  • what you have eaten today and this week, and the items in your fridge list.

These categories are sent only if you turn them on in AI data-sharing settings. All four are off until you choose:

  • medications and supplements;
  • blood-work results and medical nutrient limits;
  • menstrual cycle information (also withheld whenever Privacy Mode is on in female-health settings);
  • Apple Health data: steps, active energy, nutrition totals and weight.

You can change these settings or withdraw at any time in Settings > AI Data Sharing; changes apply to future requests. Our servers also drop any category you have not allowed before a request reaches the AI provider.

How the AI provider handles requests

The provider processes each request only to return a response. We use providers that do not use your requests to train their models and do not keep them after responding, except briefly where required to prevent abuse or meet legal obligations.

AI output can be wrong. It is not medical advice.

4. Services the app calls

Called directly from the app

These services receive your request directly from your device, so they also see your IP address. They do not receive your name, email or user ID from us.

  • Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org) and Open Beauty Facts (world.openbeautyfacts.org): the barcode you scan or the text you search.
  • TheMealDB (themealdb.com): recipe, ingredient, category or cuisine search text.
  • USDA Organic Integrity Database (organic.ams.usda.gov): a product name, to check organic certification.
  • EWG Skin Deep (ewg.org) and INCIDecoder (incidecoder.com): the personal-care product name you search.
  • Apple: location geocoding and WeatherKit (coordinates or ZIP code), Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases, and Apple Health on your device.

Called through our Cloud Functions

These requests go to our servers first, which add our API key and pass on only the fields listed. The provider does not receive your name, email or user ID from us.

  • AI provider: see section 3.
  • USDA FoodData Central: food search text or a food ID.
  • Foursquare Places: your coordinates, a search radius, an optional search term and place categories, to find nearby stores and restaurants.
  • Google Air Quality: coordinates for your area (from your ZIP code or location) to look up air quality.
  • Apple App Store: purchase receipts and transaction IDs, to verify subscriptions.

Infrastructure

Google Firebase and Google Cloud host your account, database, photos, Cloud Functions and logs. Google Sign-In is used if you choose to sign in with Google.

Links you open

Some buttons open Amazon, Instacart or other websites with the product name in the link. Those sites' own privacy policies apply once you open them.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transaction subject to appropriate safeguards. We do not sell personal information, share health information for advertising, or use personal information for cross-app tracking.

5. Storage and retention

  • On your device: preferences, caches and locally saved history stay until you delete them in the app, clear the cache, or uninstall the app.
  • Account data (profile, health information, scans, meals, workouts, favorites, shopping lists, fridge, cycle data, contributions and synced photos): kept while your account exists. Deleting your account in the app deletes this data, the photos stored under your account, and your sign-in record. We do not keep backups of the database, so deleted data cannot be restored (the database keeps prior versions of changed data for at most one hour).
  • Photos sent only for analysis (label and meal-recognition photos you do not save): not stored by us after the response. Google's handling is described in section 3.
  • AI chat history: stored only on your device (your last 20 conversations and a short summary used to keep answers consistent), not on our servers. It is removed when you sign out or delete your account. Each request sends your last few messages to the AI provider as described in section 3.
  • Cloud Functions logs (user ID, tier, request sizes, purchase IDs; never the content of your requests or AI answers): kept for 7 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Purchase and transaction records: Apple processes all payments; we never receive your payment details. We keep your App Store transaction IDs and subscription status with your account to provide access, and delete them when you delete your account. Apple keeps its own payment records under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Usage counters for monthly AI and API allowances: kept with your account, one record per month, and deleted when you delete your account.
  • Directly called services (Open Food Facts and others in section 4): governed by each service's own policy.

6. Research use (opt-in)

We use your data for research only if you turn on "Contribute to research" in the app's Privacy & Data settings. It is off by default, separate from accepting the Terms, and you can turn it off at any time. Apple Health (HealthKit) data is never used for research, even if you opt in. We record your choice with the version of the consent text you saw.

Yammoing does not currently use any data for research; the setting only records your choice for the future. Before any research use starts, we will update this policy to describe what data is used, how it is de-identified, who receives it and how long it is kept, and ask you again.

7. Your choices and rights

You can manage Apple Health, Photos, Camera, Notifications and Location permissions in iOS Settings. In the app you can change AI data-sharing settings, research opt-in and location settings, correct your profile, delete history, and permanently delete your account and associated server data. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete or export personal information and to object to or restrict certain processing.

California residents may exercise applicable CCPA rights, including the right to know, delete, correct, and receive equal service. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are used for cross-context behavioral advertising. EEA and UK users may exercise applicable GDPR rights; our legal bases include consent, contract performance, legitimate interests, and legal compliance.

8. Children

Yammoing is intended for users age 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 in violation of applicable law. Contact us if you believe a child provided information inappropriately.

9. International processing and security

Information may be processed in the United States and other locations where our providers operate. We use authentication, App Check, access rules that limit each account to its own data, encrypted network transport, server-side entitlement checks, and other safeguards appropriate to the information processed. No system can guarantee absolute security.
