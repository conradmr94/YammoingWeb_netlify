Subscriptions

Which plans are there?

Yammoing offers Standard and Premium subscriptions, billed monthly or annually. Premium adds the AI features: meal photo recognition, label analysis and the AI nutritionist. Prices for your country are shown in the app before you subscribe.

How does billing work?

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through your Apple Account. They renew automatically at the end of each period unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the period ends. If a free trial is offered, you are charged when the trial ends unless you cancel before then.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

  1. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions.
  2. Tap Yammoing.
  3. Choose another plan, or tap Cancel Subscription.

You can also open this screen from the app: Settings > Subscription > Manage in App Store. After you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com, or, for an active Premium subscription, from the app in Settings > Subscription > Request a Refund.

Restoring purchases

I already subscribed. Why does the app ask me to subscribe again?

This can happen on a new device, after reinstalling, or if you are signed in to a different Apple Account. To restore your subscription:

  1. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you used to subscribe.
  2. On any subscription screen in the app, tap Restore Purchases. It is also in Settings > Subscription.
  3. If asked, sign in to your Apple Account so the App Store can check your purchases.

If your subscription still does not appear, check that it is active in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions on your iPhone, then contact us.

Deleting your account

How do I delete my account and data?

  1. In the app, open Settings.
  2. Under Safety & Privacy, tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. This permanently deletes your Yammoing account and its cloud and local data, including scans, meals, favorites, shopping lists and female health data. It cannot be undone.

Does deleting my account cancel my subscription?

No. Apple bills subscriptions, so cancel separately in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions on your iPhone to stop future charges.
