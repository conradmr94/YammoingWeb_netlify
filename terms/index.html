Yammoing's health scores are calculated using deterministic algorithms, NOT artificial intelligence or machine learning. Health scores are computed based on:

  • Published nutritional guidelines and research (Food Compass 2.0, Nutri-Score, etc.)
  • Product nutritional data (calories, macronutrients, micronutrients)
  • Ingredient analysis against known additive and ingredient databases
  • Your personal health profile and dietary preferences (if provided)

These calculations are rule-based and reproducible. The same product will receive the same score given the same inputs and algorithm version.

28.2 Artificial Intelligence Usage

Yammoing uses a third-party AI provider, reached only through our servers, for:

  • Meal Image Recognition: meal photos you analyze are sent to the AI provider to identify food items.
  • Label Analysis: nutrition-label photos you analyze are sent to the AI provider to read them.
  • Nutrition Questions, AI Nutritionist, Trainer and Recipes: your messages are sent to the AI provider together with context about you (such as diet, allergies and goals) and any health details your AI data-sharing settings allow (such as medications, lab results, cycle data and Apple Health data).

AI output:

  • May be inaccurate or incomplete
  • Should be verified by you before logging or acting on it
  • Is not medical advice

Yammoing does NOT use AI for:

  • Generating health scores or ratings (these are rule-based; see 28.1)
  • Diagnosing, treating or predicting medical conditions

28.3 Algorithmic Limitations

YOU ACKNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTAND THAT:

  • Algorithms are based on general nutritional science and may not account for your specific circumstances
  • Health scores are estimates and simplifications of complex nutritional information
  • Different scoring methodologies may produce different results for the same product
  • Algorithms are updated periodically, which may change scores for previously scanned products
  • No algorithm can replace professional medical or nutritional advice

28.4 Data Processing

When you use the Application:

  • Barcode scans are processed to retrieve product information from databases (primarily Open Food Facts)
  • Meal and label images you analyze are sent to the AI provider (see 28.2)
  • Your health profile is processed on your device and on our servers to personalize scores
  • All processing is subject to the Privacy Policy (Section 26)

29. RESEARCH USE OF DATA (OPT-IN)

  • Your data is used for research only if you turn on "Contribute to research" in Settings > Privacy & Data. It is off by default and is not part of accepting these Terms.
  • You can turn it off at any time. Your choice is recorded with the version of the consent text you saw.
  • Apple Health (HealthKit) data is never used for research, even if you opt in.
  • Yammoing does not currently use any data for research. Before any research use begins, the Privacy Policy will describe what data is used, how it is de-identified, who receives it, and how long it is kept.
